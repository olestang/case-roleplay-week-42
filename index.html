ROLEPLAY ACTIVITY

One situation. Different perspectives.

Everyone in your group enters the same kindergarten situation, but each person has experienced it differently. Read only your own story, then talk together and find out what happened.

1Choose the same group size and case.
2Each person chooses a different role.
3You may choose different English levels.

STEP 1

How many people are in your group?

This choice decides which cases and roles you can use.

STEP 2

Choose your case.

Everyone in the group should choose the same case.

STEP 3

Choose your role.

Everyone must choose differently. Tell each other only the role name you selected—not what your story says.

STEP 4

Choose your English level.

Your group does not need to choose the same level. The events and information are the same at every level. Only the English changes.
🔒 PRIVATE · ONLY READ YOUR OWN ROLE

Read before you speak. This is what your character knows, remembers and believes. Other characters may remember things differently.

When everyone is ready

Put the stories away and play out the conversation in English. Do not read your story aloud. Listen, ask questions and react as your character would.

🙈

Your story is hidden

Pass the device safely or wait until you can read privately.
